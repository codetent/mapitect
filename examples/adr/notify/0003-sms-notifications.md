# Send shipping updates by SMS

## Context and Problem Statement

Customers wanted to know when their parcel ships. Should we send SMS?

## Decision Outcome

We sent SMS shipping updates through SendGrid's SMS partner for six months.

### Consequences

* Bad, because costs per message were high and few customers opted in.

## More Information

Deprecated in March 2026: the carrier's own tracking e-mails replaced the SMS updates.
